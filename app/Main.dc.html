Data & sources: Sun/Earth geometry computed in-app (Meeus lunar & solar series, IAU WGCCRE Moon orientation, Moon ME frame), checkable against NASA/JPL Horizons. Terrain is a procedural demo (16 × 16 km, 67 m grid, observer 2 m) pending a LOLA DEM from NASA Goddard PGDA. Site coordinates: DLR Connecting Ridge study, LROC; ≈ marks approximate values. Font: Inter (SIL OFL). No external code libraries.

Positions use a low-precision analytic ephemeris (Meeus series + IAU Moon rotation, TT = UTC + 69.184 s). Azimuth is clockwise from north; longitudes are east-positive. Sunlight is not panel power, Earth in view is not a guaranteed radio link, and this is not a landing-safety assessment.
